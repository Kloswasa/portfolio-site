## prose

Thai H asks a visual question: how do you communicate aroma without sound or scent? The book maps ingredients, lemongrass, kaffir lime, galangal, jasmine, coconut, and others, that define Thai fragrance culture.

Every spread is original: no stock illustration, no borrowed icons. The goal was a cohesive editorial object where graphic rhythm and hand-drawn detail carry the sensory story.

## prose

Each ingredient received a profile spread: botanical illustration, aroma note diagram, cultural context sidebar, and a graphic pattern derived from the plant's natural geometry. A shared colour system ties herbal, floral, and woody families across the book.

## process

- num: "01"
  title: Research + outline
  body: Eighteen ingredients selected for aroma significance, grouped by scent
    family.
- num: "02"
  title: Illustration pass
  body: Hand-drawn botanicals digitised with consistent line language and two ink
    colours.
- num: "03"
  title: Layout + pattern
  body: Grid, typography, and derived geometric patterns built per ingredient
    chapter.
